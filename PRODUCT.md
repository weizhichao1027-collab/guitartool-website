# GuitarTool website

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

Guitar and ukulele players comparing an offline practice app, existing users looking for help, and teachers or writers seeking accurate product material. The public website explains the shipped app, links to its App Store listing, and offers browser tools and practical guides.

## Brand personality

Focused, clear, playful. Preserve the established GuitarTool identity and actual app imagery. Source: README.md, MARKETING_HANDOFF.md and the existing homepages.

## Anti-references

No invented screenshots, ratings, reviews, performance claims, launch status or theme pricing. No generic stock guitar imagery replacing the authored instrument interfaces. Do not describe unreleased Android builds as downloadable.

## Design principles

- Show actual product screens and identify historical media.
- Keep download, support and privacy paths easy to find.
- Distinguish free tools from separately purchased appearances.
- Match visible facts, structured data, localization and actual release evidence.
- Preserve the existing visual system and browser-tool behavior during content releases.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Retain semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard focus, reduced motion and readable prose. Check 375, 768 and 1440px layouts, plus right-to-left Arabic. This document records existing project requirements; it does not assert complete accessibility certification.
